Trip Research

Italy has been one place on my bucket list of places to visit. On the official Italy tourism website (opens in a new tab), it contains information on various experiences, such as food tastings. Additionally, there are many places to visit, such as The Coloseum in Rome, Vatican City, and Pompeii. With so many places to visit and experiences to try, it can be hard to narrow down what events to partake in. Fortunantely, with Tripadvisor (opens in a new tab), you can read reviews from other people so you can make the right choice. Before going anywhere, it is important to know the weather so your vacation doesn't get ruined; always check the Weather App (opens in a new tab) for Rome or wherever in Italy you might want to visit.

Getting There & Staying There

Flight & Hotel Booking

To make travel and lodging easy, use Expedia (opens in a new tab) to bundle them together. To find the best prices for hotels, use Booking.com (opens in a new tab) to find the best prices.

Transportation Info

For info on busses, trains, and other ways of transport throughout the city, using Moovit.com (opens in a new tab) is a great way to see what times and routes are available. You can also use Google Maps (opens in a new tab) to see how far locations are from one another, plus how long it would take to get from place to place.
