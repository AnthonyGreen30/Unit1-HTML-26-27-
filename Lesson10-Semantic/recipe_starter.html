Grilled Cheese

Ingredients·Steps

Ingredients

  • Two loaves of bread
  • Two slices of cheese
  • Two tablespoons of soft butter
  • Any additionally ingredients you wish to add

Steps

  1. Preheat a pot, pan, or other cooking utensil above a stove
  2. Spread the butter on one side of both slices of bread
  3. Place one slice of bread in the pan, with the butter side down on the pan. Then, put pn the slice of cheese, any topings you want to add, and the other slice of cheese together. Once that's done, put the last slice of bread on top, with the butter side facing up towards you.
  4. Cook over the stove until one side of the grilled cheese is a golden brown color, then flip the grilled cheese and keep cooking until the cheese is melty.
  5. Put the grilled cheese on a plate, and after it cools enjoy the grilled cheese.

I chose the grilled cheese because it is easy to make (I don't really know how to cook anything, but a grilled cheese is simple to make), and it is also delicious.


Fun fact: Americans eat over 2 billion grilled cheeses a year.
