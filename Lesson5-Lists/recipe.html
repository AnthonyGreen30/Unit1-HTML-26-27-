Grilled Cheese

I picked the grilled cheese for a few reasons. First, grilled cheese is a classic with many different variations (one such variation I will walk you through today). Next, it is extremely easy to make; so if you are a beginner (like me), you don't have to worry about making a mistake. Additionally, expert chefs can make their own twist to this recipe and truly make it their own. Finally, it always tastes delicious.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 tablespoons of softened butter
  • 2 slices of your favorite cheese
  • A pinch of salt and pepper
  • (Optional) 2 slices of tomato
  • (Optional) 1 tablespoon of mustard

Steps

  1. Preheat the pan or whatever you have available to cook a grilled cheese on.
  2. Spread the butter one side of the two slices of bread.
  3. Put one slice of bread with the butter facing down in the pan. Afterwards, put on the cheese, sprinkle on salt and pepper, put the tomatoes on, and finally put on the last slice of bread with the butter facing up.
  4. Cook the grilled cheese over the stove until the bottom is golden brown, then flip to cook the other side until the other side is crisp. At this point, the cheese should be melty, but if not then continue to cook for an extra minute.
  5. Transfer the grilled cheese to a plate and let it coll for few minutes before eating.

Notes

  • Equipment
    • Pan or other cooking utensil
    • Stove
  • Substitutions
    • You can substitute the tomatoes for any other ingredient you choose. As a rule of thumb, anything that can go on a burger can go on a grilled cheese.
